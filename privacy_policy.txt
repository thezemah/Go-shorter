Privacy Policy for Go-shorter
Last updated: 2026-05-06

Go-shorter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All shortlinks you create are stored exclusively on your local device using
Chrome's storage.local API. No data is sent to any external server. No
analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind is performed.

You can export your shortlinks at any time as a JSON file and delete them
permanently through the Manage page or by uninstalling the extension.

If you have questions, contact: thehubeza@gmail.com
